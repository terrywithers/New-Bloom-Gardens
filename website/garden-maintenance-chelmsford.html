Most people come to us after a gardener stopped turning up, or after the garden got away from them over one busy summer. Regular maintenance stops that happening. We come on a fixed round, do the same jobs to the same standard each time, and send you photos so you know it's been done, even if you're at work or away.

What's included on a visit

  • Lawn mowing, with edges cut and strimmed
  • Weeding of beds, borders, paths and patios
  • Light pruning and deadheading through the season
  • Leaf and debris blowing, so you're left with a clean finish
  • All green waste taken away under our Upper Tier waste carrier licence

How often?

Weekly suits larger lawns in the growing season. Fortnightly is the most popular choice for a typical Chelmsford garden. Monthly works for low-maintenance gardens or through the winter. You can change frequency with the seasons, and there's no long contract.

Extras when you need them

Because we're already there, it's easy to add hedge cutting, lawn treatments, leaf clearance in autumn or gutter clearing. We'll always price extras before doing them.

Areas we cover

We're based just outside Chelmsford and work across Chelmsford, Great Waltham, Broomfield, Writtle, Great Baddow, Springfield, Danbury, Boreham, Little Waltham, Witham, Maldon and South Woodham Ferrers. For larger jobs and commercial contracts we also cover the rest of Essex and East London.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does regular garden maintenance cost?

It depends on the size of the garden and how often we visit. We give a fixed price per visit before we start, often from a few photos sent on WhatsApp, so there are no surprises.

Do I need to be home when you visit?

No. As long as we can get into the garden, we'll do the work and send you before and after photos when we finish.

Do you take the garden waste away?

Yes. All cuttings and green waste are removed and disposed of responsibly under our Environment Agency Upper Tier waste carrier registration, CBDU656246.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Pick weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits and change it whenever you need to.
