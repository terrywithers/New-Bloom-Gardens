Services

Everything outside, on one contract

1

Scheduled grounds maintenance

Mowing, edging, strimming, weeding and blowing on weekly, fortnightly or monthly cycles, set around the growing season.

2

Hedge & shrub management

Maintenance cuts, height reductions and reshaping, timed with regard to nesting birds. All arisings removed.

3

Lawn renovation

Scarification, aeration, overseeding, feeding and returfing to bring tired or drought-damaged lawns back.

4

Site clearance & vegetation reduction

Overgrown plots, neglected gardens and blocked access paths cut back and cleared. Ideal for void and probate properties.

5

Seasonal & winter services

Leaf clearance, gutter clearing, gritting, winter hedge cutting and pre-spring tidy-ups to keep sites safe and presentable year-round.

6

Green waste removal

Taken away and disposed of responsibly under our Environment Agency Upper Tier waste carrier registration. No piles left on site.

How we work

Simple to instruct. Easy to audit.

  1. Site survey

    We walk the site, note access, hazards and priorities, and agree what "good" looks like.

  2. Fixed, itemised quote

    A written specification and price per visit or per job. No surprises on the invoice.

  3. Agreed schedule

    Visit frequency set around the seasons, with one named contact for the account.

  4. Report every visit

    A photo completion report after each visit, ready to share with clients or residents.
