## The big idea

"Honest and reliable" is what every customer wants, and almost nobody can prove it. So we prove it instead of saying it:

> **"You'll always know your garden's been done."**
> We send photos after every visit. You get the same team each time, clear pricing and no surprises.

This line works for every type of customer:

| Customer | What actually worries them | How New Bloom solves it |
|---|---|---|
| Homeowners | Gardeners who don't turn up or cut corners | Booked-in visits, same team, before/after photos |
| Property & block managers | Contractors who need chasing; leaseholders complaining | Photo report after every visit, RAMS & insurance on file, one monthly invoice |
| Probate solicitors / executors | Empty houses with overgrown gardens that hurt the sale price and invite break-ins | One-off clearance, then upkeep until sale, with photo proof for the file. Executors who live far away never need to visit |
| Letting agents / landlords | End-of-tenancy gardens, deposit disputes | Fast turnaround, dated photos for the inventory |

**Probate solicitors and block managers are your best opportunity.** Each one can send repeat work for years, competitors rarely approach them, and it costs nothing to contact them. Most of the budget for this plan is your time, and it should go here.

## Priority order (do in this order)

### Week 1: Fix the foundations (Google)
1. **Check the website on your phone.** Make sure `www.newbloomgardens.co.uk` loads quickly, shows the phone number at the top, and has a working quote form. Search Google for `site:newbloomgardens.co.uk`. If nothing comes up, Google hasn't indexed the site yet, so set up Google Search Console (see `02-website-seo.md`).
2. **Complete your Google Business Profile.** See `01-google-business-profile.md`. This is the single biggest free lead source for a local trade.
3. **Get 10 Google reviews from existing customers.** Use the scripts in `01-google-business-profile.md`. Reviews make you rank higher *and* convert better.

### Weeks 2–4: Start the B2B pipeline
4. **Build a list of 50 targets:** probate solicitors, block and property managers, and letting agents in your area. How to find them is in `03-b2b-outreach.md`.
5. **Send 10 outreach emails a week** using the templates, and follow up after 5 working days.
6. **Attach your Commercial Brochure** (already done, and it's strong) plus one anonymised completion report to every outreach email. See `05-audit-of-current-material.md` before sending any report as a sample.

### Weeks 2–12: Social media on autopilot
7. **Post 3x a week** using the 30-day calendar in `04-social-content-calendar.md`. Batch-write a week of posts in one sitting.
8. **Join 5–10 local Facebook community groups** (e.g. "Chelmsford Community", "Romford Residents") and follow each group's rules on business posts. Many allow them on a set day.
9. **Set up a free Nextdoor business page** and ask happy customers to recommend you there.

### Month 2–3: Website update
10. **Launch the new website** in `/website` (home, commercial and booking pages). See `website/README.md` for going live and connecting the form to your email. Add area pages later.

## How to spend the £50 a month

| Spend | Approx. cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Boost your best Facebook/Instagram post** (only one that has already done well organically), targeted 5–10 miles around your base, homeowners 35+ | £20–£30 | Cheapest way to reach local homeowners |
| **A6 leaflets** (one-off print run of ~1,000, from an online printer such as Solopress or Vistaprint) | roughly £20–£40 one-off | Put them through doors on roads next to jobs you're already doing ("We're working on your road…") |
| **£0: Don't buy** Checkatrade, Bark, MyBuilder or Google Ads yet | n/a | At this budget they will burn through the money with little return. Revisit once reviews and the website are sorted |

**Leaflet trick:** whenever you're working on a street, post leaflets through 20–30 neighbouring doors that day: *"We're looking after a garden on your road. Want us to quote for yours while we're here?"* This is the best-converting leaflet a gardener can use, because the neighbours can see the work.
