# Getting found on Google

**Where you are (Oct 2026):** your Google profile is complete with 15 reviews, but in six months fewer than 50 searches showed it. Most people find you by searching your name. The goal is to appear when people search **"gardener Chelmsford"**, **"garden clearance Chelmsford"**, **"hedge cutting near me"** and similar.

Google ranks local businesses on three things: **relevance** (does your site and profile clearly match the search?), **distance**, and **prominence** (reviews, links, mentions around the web). Distance you can't change, so the work goes into the other two.

## Every week (about 15 minutes)
- **One Google post** with a before/after photo from that week's job, linking to the matching service page. For example, a clearance job post links to `garden-clearance-chelmsford.html`.
- **Upload 3–5 job photos** to the Google profile.
- **Ask every happy customer for a review.** Ask them to say *what we did and where*, e.g. "hedge cutting in Broomfield". Mentions of services and places in reviews help you rank for them. Don't write it for them, and never offer anything in return.
- **Reply to every review**, mentioning the job and area naturally.

Target: **2–4 new reviews a month**, steadily. A regular flow counts for more than a burst.
