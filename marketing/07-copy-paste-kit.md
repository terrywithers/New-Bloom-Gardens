## Reviews

Get your review link from your Google profile (Ask for reviews → copy link) and paste it where it says [REVIEW LINK].

**Review request: text or WhatsApp** _(Send the same day as the job)_

```
Hi [Name], thanks for having us today! If you're happy with the work, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps a small local business. It'd be great if you could mention what we did and where (e.g. hedge cutting in Broomfield). [REVIEW LINK] Thanks, [Your name], New Bloom Gardens
```

**Review request: commercial email** _(Email to managers and solicitors)_

```
Subject: A quick favour

Hi [Name],

Thanks for continuing to trust us with [site]. If you've been happy with our service, a short Google review would really help other managers find us: [REVIEW LINK]

Even a sentence or two helps. Many thanks,
[Your name]
New Bloom Gardens · 07504 548304
```

**Gentle reminder (a week later)** _(Only once, only if no review yet)_

```
Hi [Name], just a quick nudge in case the review link got lost: [REVIEW LINK] No worries at all if not. Thanks again! [Your name]
```

**Reply to a 5-star review** _(Edit [Name], job and village each time)_

```
Thanks so much, [Name]! It was a pleasure doing the [job] at your place in [village]. See you on the next visit. [Your name], New Bloom Gardens
```

**Reply to a critical review** _(Reply within 24 hours, stay calm)_

```
Hi [Name], I'm sorry to hear this. It's not the standard we aim for. Please call me on 07504 548304 so I can understand what happened and put it right. [Your name], New Bloom Gardens
```

## Messages to existing customers

Upselling current customers is what works best for you. Save your customers in a WhatsApp broadcast list (Chats → ⋯ → New broadcast) and send these. Only people who have your number saved will receive broadcasts.

**Winter services (send early November)** _(WhatsApp broadcast, with the Winter flyer image)_

```
Hi, it's New Bloom Gardens 🌱 We're booking winter jobs now: gutter clearing & fascia wash from £80, leaf clearance from £60, plus hedge cutting and moss/driveway treatment. Reply here if you'd like a price or a date. Thanks for being a customer!
```

**Lawn rescue (send now, and again in March)** _(WhatsApp broadcast, with the Lawn Rescue flyer)_

```
Hi, New Bloom Gardens here 🌱 After the hot summer, now's the best time to get lawns back: scarifying, aeration, overseeding or new turf. Reply with a photo of your lawn and we'll send you a price.
```

**Turn a one-off job into regular visits** _(Send 2 weeks after a one-off tidy)_

```
Hi [Name], hope the garden's still looking good! If you'd like to keep it that way, we can pop in fortnightly or monthly at a fixed price per visit, with no contract. Want me to send you a price?
```

**"While we're here" add-on** _(Add to the completion report message)_

```
While we were there we noticed [the gutters / the front hedge / the patio] could do with some attention. Happy to quote if you'd like. Just reply to this message.
```
