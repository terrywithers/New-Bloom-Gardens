# B2B Outreach: Probate Solicitors, Block & Property Managers, Letting Agents

**Why this matters:** one block manager can look after 20+ sites, and one probate solicitor handles a steady stream of empty houses. A single "yes" here can be worth more than 20 homeowner leads. It costs nothing but time.

**Target:** 10 emails a week, a follow-up after 5 working days, and a second follow-up after another 10. Most replies come from the follow-ups, not the first email.

---

## Where to find targets (free)

| Target | Where to look |
|---|---|
| **Probate solicitors** | Law Society "Find a Solicitor" (search "Wills and probate" by Essex/London postcode) · Google Maps: "probate solicitor Chelmsford" etc. · STEP directory (private client practitioners) |
| **Block managers** | ARMA (Association of Residential Managing Agents) member directory · Property Institute · Google Maps: "block management Essex" · Look at the signage on local blocks of flats; it names the managing agent |
| **Property managers / commercial** | Google Maps: "property management company [town]" · Local business parks (ask reception who manages the site) · Facilities management firms (they subcontract grounds work) |
| **Letting agents** | Rightmove/Zoopla agent listings for your towns · High street agents (drop in with a leaflet) |
| **Estate agents** | They see overgrown probate and empty houses before anyone else. Offer "kerb appeal" tidy-ups before photos and viewings |

**Get the right person:** call reception and ask *"Who looks after appointing contractors for grounds maintenance?"* or *"Who in the probate team deals with property?"*. Then email that person by name.

Keep a simple tracker: `Company | Contact | Email | Phone | Date sent | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Outcome`.

## Rules (UK GDPR / PECR)
- Emailing a **business contact at a company** about a relevant service is generally allowed. Keep it relevant, say who you are, and remove anyone who asks.
- Don't send marketing emails to **individuals' personal emails** without consent.
- Add a line to every email: *"If you'd rather not hear from me, just reply and let me know."*
