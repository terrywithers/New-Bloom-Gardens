# Social Media: 30-Day Content Calendar (mid-Oct to mid-Nov)

**Platforms:** Facebook + Instagram (post once and share to both via Meta Business Suite, free). Copy each one to Google Business Profile as a weekly update too.
**Frequency:** 3 posts a week (Mon / Wed / Fri) plus Instagram Stories on job days.
**Time it takes:** about 1 hour a week. Write the week's posts in one sitting and schedule them in Meta Business Suite.

## Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 🔄 **Before & after** | 40% | Your strongest content. Same angle, same spot, before and after. |
| 💡 **Helpful tips** | 25% | Seasonal advice that shows you know your stuff |
| 🤝 **Trust & people** | 20% | The team, the van, reviews, "we turned up in the rain" |
| 📣 **Offer / call to action** | 15% | Direct asks: "Booking autumn tidy-ups now" |

### Photo rules (these matter most)
- **Take before/after photos on every job**, from the same spot at the same angle, phone held horizontally for Facebook and vertically for Reels.
- Short **Reels (7–15 seconds)** of a before-to-after transition reach far more people than photos. Use the free CapCut app with a "transition" template.
- Never show house numbers, car number plates or clients' faces without permission.

## Free ways to get more reach
1. **Local Facebook groups:** share the best before/after posts in community groups on the days they allow business posts. Never just drop a link; post the photos and a friendly line.
2. **Tag the area:** add a location to every Instagram post (town or neighbourhood, never the exact address).
3. **Reply to every comment** within a few hours. It boosts the post in the algorithm.
4. **Ask happy customers to share** your before/after post of their garden. Their friends are your best prospects.
5. **Pin a post** to the top of your Facebook and Instagram profiles: your best before/after, with "Free quotes across Essex & East London".
