# Google Business Profile & Reviews

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is what shows in the map results when someone searches "gardener near me" or "grounds maintenance Chelmsford". For a local service business it usually brings in more leads than the website.

Log in at **business.google.com** and work through this checklist.

---

## 1. Profile checklist

- [ ] **Business name:** `New Bloom Gardens`. Don't add keywords like "New Bloom Gardens – Gardener Essex"; Google can suspend profiles for that.
- [ ] **Primary category:** `Gardener`
- [ ] **Additional categories:** add whichever of these appear in Google's list and match work you actually do: `Lawn care service`, `Landscaper`, `Hedge trimming service`, `Tree service`
- [ ] **Service-area business:** hide your home address and list up to 20 service areas. Suggested list, starting closest to Chelmsford:
  - **Essex:** Chelmsford, Great Baddow, Writtle, Danbury, Witham, Maldon, South Woodham Ferrers, Billericay, Brentwood, Basildon, Wickford, Romford, Hornchurch, Upminster
  - **East London:** Havering, Redbridge, Ilford, Barking and Dagenham, Waltham Forest, Newham
- [ ] **Hours:** set them accurately, and add "special hours" for bank holidays
- [ ] **Phone & website:** use `https://www.newbloomgardens.co.uk`, written exactly the same way everywhere
- [ ] **Services:** add every service as a separate item with a short description (list below)
- [ ] **Photos:** upload **at least 20** to start, then **2–3 every week**. Before/after photos, the team in uniform, the van and tidy finished jobs. Profiles with lots of recent photos get more clicks.
- [ ] **Logo & cover photo:** use your logo, and a finished job as the cover
- [ ] **Business description:** paste the text below
- [ ] **Messaging:** turn it on, and reply within a few hours

### Business description (750 characters max; paste as is)

> New Bloom Gardens provides honest, reliable garden and grounds maintenance across Essex and East London. We look after private gardens for homeowners, and communal grounds for block managers, property managers, letting agents and probate solicitors.
>
> Our services include regular garden maintenance, lawn mowing, hedge cutting, garden clearances, seasonal tidy-ups, communal grounds maintenance and upkeep of empty properties.
>
> You get the same team each visit, clear pricing, and before-and-after photos after every job, so you always know the work's been done. Fully insured, and we hold an upper-tier waste carrier licence. Commercial clients receive RAMS and consolidated monthly invoicing.
>
> Call or message for a free, no-obligation quote.

*(Edit the insurance line if you don't hold it, and never claim anything you can't back up.)*

- Regular garden maintenance (weekly / fortnightly / monthly)

- Garden clearance (one-off)

GBP lets you publish "Updates". Post one a week. Recycle your social posts (see `04-social-content-calendar.md`), always with a photo and a "Call now" or "Get a quote" button.

## 3. Getting reviews: the system

Reviews are the biggest ranking factor you control. **Aim for 25+ in 90 days, then a steady 2–4 a month.**

**Get your review link:** in GBP, click *"Ask for reviews"* to copy a short link. Turn it into a QR code (free at many QR generator sites) for leaflets and invoices.

### When to ask
Right after a job when the customer is happy, while they're still looking at the garden. Ask the same day, by text or WhatsApp.

### Text/WhatsApp script (existing customers)
> Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from New Bloom Gardens. Thanks again for having us look after your garden. We're a small local business and Google reviews really help us. If you have 30 seconds, would you mind leaving us a quick one? https://g.page/r/CbyCRGgCzelEEBM/review Thank you!

### Commercial clients (email)
> Subject: A quick favour
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for continuing to trust us with [site name]. If you've been happy with our service, a short Google review would really help other managers find us: https://g.page/r/CbyCRGgCzelEEBM/review
>
> Even one sentence helps. Many thanks,
> [Your name]
> New Bloom Gardens

### Reply to every review
- **Good review:** thank them by first name and mention the job and the area, e.g. *"Thanks Sarah, it was a pleasure getting your Chelmsford garden ready for winter."* Naturally mentioning a place and a service helps local search.
- **Bad review:** reply calmly within 24 hours, don't argue, and take it offline: *"Sorry to hear this, [Name]. Please call me on 07504 548304 so I can put it right."*

### Rules
- Don't offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews (against Google's policy).
- Don't write fake reviews or have friends who aren't customers post them; Google can remove reviews or suspend the profile.

## 4. Free directory listings (citations)

Listing the business with the **exact same name, phone and website** on other sites helps Google trust and rank you. All of these have free listings:

- [ ] Bing Places for Business (can import straight from Google)
- [ ] Apple Business Connect (Apple Maps / Siri)
- [ ] Nextdoor Business page
- [ ] Yell.com
- [ ] Thomson Local
- [ ] FreeIndex
- [ ] Cylex UK
- [ ] Hotfrog UK
- [ ] Facebook page (make sure name, phone and website match exactly)
- [ ] Your local council's business directory, or the local Chamber of Commerce if you join

Keep the name, address and phone identical everywhere, down to the formatting.
