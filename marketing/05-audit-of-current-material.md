## Lean into what's working: upselling existing customers
You said upselling current customers works best. Make it a system instead of something that happens ad hoc:
1. **Seasonal message to every customer** (WhatsApp broadcast list, free): the Winter flyer goes out in early November, Lawn Rescue in March and September, hedge cutting in late August.
2. **"While we're here" line on every completion report:** *"We noticed your gutters / fence-line hedge / patio could do with attention. Reply to this message for a price."*
3. **Referral reward:** *"Refer a neighbour who books regular maintenance and get your next visit free"* (or £20 off). Printed on the completion report and sent by WhatsApp. Neighbour referrals are how you started, according to the brochure.
4. **Convert one-off jobs to regular ones:** two weeks after every one-off tidy, send *"Want to keep it looking like this? Fortnightly visits from £[X]."*
