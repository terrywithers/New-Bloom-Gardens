# Website & SEO Plan: newbloomgardens.co.uk

The website has one job: **turn people who find you on Google into quote requests.** It needs to be quick to load, work well on a phone, and make calling or getting a quote obvious on every page.

---

## Recommended site structure

```
Home
├── Residential Garden Maintenance
├── Commercial Grounds Maintenance
├── Block & Property Management     ← dedicated page for managing agents
├── Probate & Empty Properties      ← dedicated page for solicitors & executors
├── Garden Clearance
├── Areas We Cover
│   ├── Chelmsford
│   ├── Brentwood
│   ├── Romford & Havering
│   ├── Basildon
│   └── (more areas over time, each with its own page)
├── Our Work (before/after gallery)
├── Reviews
└── Get a Quote (form + phone + WhatsApp)
```

**Why separate pages?** Google ranks *pages*, not websites. A page called "Communal Grounds Maintenance for Block Managers in Essex" can rank for exactly that search. A homepage that tries to cover everything ranks for none of it.

**Area pages must be genuinely different.** Copying one page 10 times and only swapping the town name can be treated as spam. Each area page should mention real roads or estates you work on, a local job with photos, and a local review.

## What every page needs

- [ ] **Phone number and "Get a Quote" button** at the top, and click-to-call on mobile
- [ ] **WhatsApp button**, because many people prefer messaging a tradesperson
- [ ] **Trust strip:** ★ Google rating · Fully insured · Photo reports every visit · Same team · Local to Essex
- [ ] **Real photos.** Avoid stock photos, which make a business look less trustworthy.
- [ ] **A review or two** relevant to that page
- [ ] **Simple quote form:** name, phone, postcode, service, "tell us about the garden", and an optional photo upload

### For the commercial / block / probate pages specifically
- [ ] Insurance level ("£1m public liability")
- [ ] RAMS available on request
- [ ] Upper-tier waste carrier licence number
- [ ] How the photo reporting works (screenshot an example)
- [ ] One monthly invoice; PO numbers welcome
- [ ] Response time promise, e.g. "Quote within 48 hours, site visit within the week"
